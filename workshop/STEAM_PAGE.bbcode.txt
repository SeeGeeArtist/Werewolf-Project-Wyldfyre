[h1]Werewolf: Project Wyldfyre[/h1]
[i]A Werewolf: The Apocalypse fan mod for Project Zomboid Build 42. Play a Garou; or hunt one.[/i]

[b]Requires [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3619862853]ZombieBuddy[/url] 2.3.2 or newer.[/b] When ZombieBuddy asks, choose Allow for the "Werewolf: Project Wyldfyre" Java module. Install notes are at the bottom.

[h2]Become a Garou[/h2]
Take the [b]Werewolf[/b] trait (8 points; Fast Healer, Hearty Appetite and Cat's Eyes come with it). On first spawn the Werewolf Startup panel asks who you are, then locks it for that character:
[list]
[*][b]Origin[/b]: Human-born starts as, and falls back to, Human; Wolf-born to Wolf; Metis to Near-Human. A Metis enters the war form at any Rage, burns it half as fast, and can leave it at 55 Rage or below. Everyone else needs 55 to enter and 75 or below to leave.
[*][b]Coat[/b]: the fur colour of your beast forms.
[*][b]Auspice[/b]: the moon you were born under sets the Rage you drift back to when fed, watered and calm (30 to 70).
[/list]

[h2]Five forms, one body each[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Human (Homid)[/b]: the person you were. Nothing marks you.
[*][b]Near-Human (Glabro)[/b]: bigger, stronger, still passes at a distance. Keeps its clothes, shoes and gloves.
[*][b]Berzerker (Crinos)[/b]: the war form. Huge, double melee damage, shrugs off most flinches, sprints through wooden doors and over the first zombies in its way. Burns Rage every second you hold it and throws you back to your home form at 0. Too big for a car seat.
[*][b]Near-Wolf (Hispo)[/b]: the dire wolf. Fast, tough, carries in its jaws, wears your bags as saddlebags on a harness. Too big for a car seat.
[*][b]Wolf (Lupus)[/b]: a true wolf. Quiet, quick, harmless-looking. Can drive, badly, as if drunk.
[/list]
Shifts are channelled and cancellable (move or Esc): six seconds near the middle of the Rage bar, one second toward either end; the war form is always a one-second snap. Beast forms tear out of whatever you wear: Strip first, Dress afterwards (the same items come back from your bags or the floor). Hats, bags, jewellery, watches and held items are never damaged.

[h2]Rage[/h2]
Rage is situational. Fear, stress, pain and taking damage raise it; dealing damage spends it; food, water and howling settle it toward your auspice baseline; sadness, exhaustion and drowsiness drain it. At 100 Rage the beast takes over: a countdown, then a forced war form. Resist (panel button, radial slice or a bound key) stops the countdown, but every Resist makes the next one shorter while Rage stays over 95. Calm down or give in.

[h2]Paws[/h2]
Near-Wolf and Wolf have no hands: no firearms (you can carry a gun, not fire, reload or rack it), no crafting, building, mechanics or siphoning. One item in the jaws, any worn bag as saddlebags. The war form keeps its thumbs. Shift back for the rest.

[h2]Healing, bites and silver[/h2]
Beast forms heal fast: the war form closes ordinary wounds in about 100 seconds, Near-Wolf and Near-Human in under four minutes, and a finished heal clears glass, bullets, stitches and pain. A zombie bite is a Wyrm infection and is purged only by time in a beast form (about five minutes in the war form). Silver is the exception to all of it: it burns wherever it touches bare skin, a silver wound stops healing until the burn is gone, and a silver-burned foot will not sprint.

[h2]For the hunters[/h2]
Human players get a real counter-kit, crafted at a counter or table unless noted:
[list]
[*][b]Silver Fragments[/b] from four pieces of silver jewellery and pliers, or one piece of silverware. A file turns a fragment into Silver Filings (20 uses).
[*][b]Silver rounds[/b] for every calibre (9mm, .45, .44, .38, .357, .30-30, .308, 5.56) and shotgun shells, queued in bulk. Loaded like any other ammunition from the gun's context menu or reload radial; a gun holds silver or ordinary rounds, never a mix. Each silver round that wounds a werewolf burns the wound it made, and one that stays in burns until it is pulled.
[*][b]Forged silver weapons[/b]: a metal melee weapon, two fragments, charcoal, hammer and tongs at a forge. Permanent; needs the Blacksmith level it takes to forge that weapon in the first place.
[*][b]Silver nitrate[/b]: fertilizer or a cold pack, a car battery, fragments and an empty glass vessel, cooked over a campfire, barbecue or stove. A litre coats five metal melee weapons anywhere. A coat burns werewolves for twelve landed hits, costs 10% condition, and the weapon's tooltip shows the hits left. Poison if drunk. Wear gloves.
[*][b]Scattered silver[/b]: throw fragments or filings on a floor square to salt a doorway. Bare feet and paws pick up fragments that burn until they are pulled out through the health panel. Werewolves smell it a few tiles away. Anyone can sweep it up.
[*][b]Silver smoke bomb[/b]: ten filings uses in a vanilla smoke bomb. Any Garou in the smoke takes a silver burn to the face and throat, can barely see, coughs and builds Rage. A gas mask with a filter keeps the dust out; humans breathe it like ordinary smoke.
[/list]
Silver inside a vessel or a gun does not burn; bare silver in the hand or against the skin does.

[h2]Multiplayer, controller, Steam Deck[/h2]
[list]
[*]Solo and multiplayer (host-and-join tested). Every player and the server need the same version; the Java supplement is client-only, the server runs the Lua.
[*]Native controller wheels for forms, Howl, Strip, Dress, Resist and the Rage panel. Split-screen supported.
[*]Steam Deck works; ZombieBuddy needs its native Linux runtime (see its page).
[/list]

[h2]Install[/h2]
[olist]
[*]Subscribe to ZombieBuddy and do its one-time install (its installer, or the launch-option method on its page).
[*]Subscribe here and enable "Werewolf: Project Wyldfyre" in the mod list.
[*]When ZombieBuddy asks about the WTAGarou Java module, choose Allow. Without it the Lua side still runs, but the per-form bodies and the war-form cosmetics do not appear.
[*]New character: take the Werewolf trait. The Werewolf Guide opens on first spawn and stays behind the Guide button on the Rage panel.
[/olist]

[h2]Known limits[/h2]
[list]
[*]Build 42 moves under us; the Java supplement targets 42.20/42.21. If an update breaks the form bodies, the Lua gameplay keeps working while we catch up.
[*]NPC reactions (witnesses, infamy, hunters) are not in this release.
[*]Other mods: report what breaks; compatibility fixes are the plan for the first updates.
[/list]
