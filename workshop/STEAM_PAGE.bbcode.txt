[h1]Werewolf: Project Wyldfyre[/h1]
[i]A Werewolf: The Apocalypse fan mod for Project Zomboid Build 42. Play as a werewolf; or have a good old werewolf hunt.[/i]

[b]Requires [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3619862853]ZombieBuddy[/url] 2.3.2 or newer.[/b] When ZombieBuddy asks, choose Allow for the "Werewolf: Project Wyldfyre" Java module. Install notes are at the bottom.

[h2]Become a Garou[/h2]
Take the [b]Werewolf[/b] trait (8 points; Fast Healer, Hearty Appetite and Cat's Eyes come with it). On first spawn the Werewolf Startup panel asks who you are, then locks your choices for that character:
[list]
[*][b]Origin[/b]: Human-born starts as, and falls back to, Human; Wolf-born to Wolf; Berzerker to Near-Human. A Berzerker can enter the berzerker form at any rage, and burns it half as fast, but can only leave it at 55 Rage or below. Everyone else needs 55 rage to go berzerk and 75 or below to leave.
[*][b]Coat[/b]: the fur colour of your beast forms.
[*][b]Baseline[/b]: the moon you were born under sets the Rage you drift back to when fed, watered and calm (30 to 70).
[/list]

[h2]Five forms[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Human (Homid)[/b]: the person you were. Nothing gives away your true nature.
[*][b]Near-Human (Glabro)[/b]: bigger, stronger, still passes at a distance. Keeps its clothes, shoes and gloves.
[*][b]Berzerker (Crinos)[/b]: the war form. Huge, double melee damage, shrugs off most flinches, sprints through wooden doors and through the first few zombies in its way. Burns rage every second you hold it and throws you back to your true form at 0 rage. Too big to enter a car.
[*][b]Near-Wolf (Hispo)[/b]: the dire wolf. Fast, tough, carries primary slot in its jaws, all bags appear as saddlebags on a harness. Too big to enter a car.
[*][b]Wolf (Lupus)[/b]: a true wolf, quiet and quick. Can enter cars and even drive, though badly, as if drunk.
[/list]
Shifts are channelled and cancellable (move or Esc): they take six seconds near the middle of the Rage bar, one second toward either end; the war form is always a one-second snap. Beast forms tear out of whatever you wear: Strip first, Dress afterwards (the same items you took off or lost to shapeshifting come back from your bags or the floor, if nearby). Hats, bags, jewellery, watches and held items are never damaged or lost by shifting.

[h2]Rage[/h2]
Rage is situational. Fear, stress, pain and taking damage raise it; dealing damage spends it; food, water and howling settle it toward your auspice baseline; sadness, exhaustion and drowsiness drain it. At 100 Rage the beast takes over after a 10-second countdown. Resist (panel button, radial slice or a bound key) stops the countdown, but every Resist makes the next countdown shorter as long as Rage stays over 95. Calm down or give in to the rage.

[h2]Paws[/h2]
Near-Wolf and Wolf have no hands, but don't get their bare feet scratched. So no firearms (you can carry a gun, but cannot fire, reload or rack it), no crafting, building, mechanics or siphoning. Primary item is carried in the jaws. Any worn bag appears as saddlebags. The berzerker form keeps its thumbs. Shift back for the rest.

[h2]Healing, bites and silver[/h2]
Beast forms heal fast: the war form closes ordinary wounds in about 100 seconds, Near-Wolf and Near-Human in under four minutes, and a finished heal clears glass, bullets, stitches and pain. A zombie bite is labelled as Wyrm infection and is purged only by time in a beast form (about five minutes in the war form). Silver is the exception to all of it: it burns wherever it touches bare skin, a silver wound stops super-healing until the burn is gone, and a silver-burned foot will not sprint.

[h2]For the hunters[/h2]
Human players get a real werewolf-slaying kit, crafted at a counter or table unless noted:
[list]
[*][b]Silver Fragments[/b] from four pieces of silver jewellery and pliers, or one piece of silverware. A file turns a fragment into Silver Filings (20 uses).
[*][b]Silver rounds[/b] for every calibre (9mm, .45, .44, .38, .357, .30-30, .308, 5.56) and shotgun shells, queued in bulk. Loaded like any other ammunition from the gun's context menu or reload radial; a gun holds silver or ordinary rounds, never a mix. Each silver round that wounds a werewolf burns the wound it made, and one that stays in burns until it is pulled.
[*][b]Forged silver weapons[/b]: a metal melee weapon, two fragments, charcoal, hammer and tongs at a forge. Permanent; needs the Blacksmith level it takes to forge that weapon in the first place.
[*][b]Silver nitrate[/b]: fertilizer or a cold pack, a car battery, fragments and an empty glass vessel, cooked over a campfire, barbecue or stove. A litre coats five metal melee weapons anywhere. A coat burns werewolves for twelve landed hits, costs 10% condition, and the weapon's tooltip shows the hits left. Poison if drunk. Wear gloves.
[*][b]Scattered silver[/b]: throw fragments or filings on a floor square to salt a doorway. Bare feet and paws pick up fragments that burn until they are pulled out through the health panel. Werewolves smell it a few tiles away. Anyone can sweep it up.
[*][b]Silver smoke bomb[/b]: ten filings uses in a vanilla smoke bomb. Any Garou in the smoke takes a silver burn to the face and throat, can barely see, coughs and builds Rage. A gas mask with a filter keeps the dust out; humans breathe it like ordinary smoke.
[/list]
Silver inside a vessel or a gun does not burn; bare silver in the hand or against the skin does.

[h2]Controller[/h2]
[list]
[*]Native controller wheels for forms, Howl, Strip, Dress, Resist and the Rage panel. Defaults are d-pad down for howl, dress, strip, and toggling the rage panel; and d-pad right for resist and shapeshifting. Split-screen supported.
[/list]

[h2]Install[/h2]
[olist]
[*]Subscribe to ZombieBuddy and do its one-time install (its installer, or the launch-option method on its page).
[*]Subscribe here and enable "Werewolf: Project Wyldfyre" in the mod list.
[*]When ZombieBuddy asks about the WTAGarou Java module, choose Allow. Without it the Lua side still runs, but the per-form bodies and the war-form cosmetics do not appear.
[*]New character: take the Werewolf trait. The Werewolf Guide opens on first spawn and stays behind the Guide button on the Rage panel.
[/olist]
